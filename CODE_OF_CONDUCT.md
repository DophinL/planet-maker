# Code of Conduct

We want Planet Maker to be a welcoming, practical place to build and learn. Be respectful, assume good intent, give actionable feedback, and do not harass or demean other contributors.

Unacceptable behavior includes discriminatory language, personal attacks, unwanted sexual attention, deliberate intimidation, publishing private information, or sustained disruption of project discussions.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior harms the community. Report concerns privately to the repository owner through the contact method on their GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed as promptly and confidentially as practical.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone is publicly representing the project.
