# Celestial texture credits

Mercury, Venus surface and atmosphere, Earth surface and clouds, Mars,
Jupiter, Saturn, Saturn rings, Uranus, Neptune, and the Moon use the Solar
System Scope texture pack by INOVE. The pack is distributed under the
Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International license.

- Source: https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/
- License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

The Pluto global color mosaic is based on imagery from NASA's New Horizons
mission and is credited to NASA/JHUAPL/SwRI. The original observed mosaic is
kept as `pluto.jpg`; `pluto-reconstructed.jpg` fills only the source's unmapped
region with an AI-assisted, scientifically plausible continuation so the map
can wrap a complete sphere without a black missing-data gap.

- Source: https://science.nasa.gov/resource/pluto-global-color-map/
