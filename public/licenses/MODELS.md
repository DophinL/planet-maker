# 3D model credits

## Tripo showcase assets

Files under `tripo-showcases/` are original assets generated for Make3DMap
through the Tripo v3 API. Their prompts, model versions, processing stages,
task IDs, checksums, sizes, and mesh statistics are recorded in
`tripo-showcases/manifest.json`. They are not third-party stock models.

The application bundles the following third-party models.
